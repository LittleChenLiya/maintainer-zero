# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want Maintainer-Zero to be a useful, respectful place for maintainers, contributors, and
people reviewing continuity risks. Participants are expected to make participation safe and
welcoming for everyone, regardless of experience, background, identity, or affiliation.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, specific, and constructive when discussing incidents, scores, or design tradeoffs.
- Focus criticism on the work and its evidence, not on people or organizations.
- Protect privacy: never publish tokens, private repository content, or unredacted personal data.
- Respect the project boundaries: reports are heuristic and must not be used to shame individual
  maintainers or to make unsupported security or compliance claims.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, deliberate exposure of private information, and
other conduct that would make a reasonable participant feel unsafe are not acceptable.

## Reporting

For conduct concerns, contact the repository owner through a private GitHub channel. Do not put
sensitive details in a public issue or Discussion. For security or privacy vulnerabilities, follow
[SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) instead.

Reports will be reviewed in good faith and handled as confidentially as the available GitHub
channels allow. Retaliation against a good-faith report is not acceptable.
